Kurukshetra is divided into several areas, each with its unique characteristics and advantages. For students interested in app development, the following areas are particularly relevant: *

Thanesar is a thriving commercial hub, home to many startups and small businesses. Pipli

The University Area is a hub for academic and research activities, with several institutions offering courses in computer science and technology. Near Railway Station

The area near the railway station is a bustling hub of activity, with many commuters and travelers passing through. This area offers a range of opportunities for app development, from creating travel apps to developing logistics and delivery platforms.
