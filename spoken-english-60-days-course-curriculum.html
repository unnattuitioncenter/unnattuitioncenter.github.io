Spoken English 60 Days Course Curriculum

Course Overview

The Spoken English 60 Days Course at Unnat Tuition Center is specifically designed for students in Kurukshetra, Haryana, to enhance their communication skills and confidently interact with locals and international visitors in the region. This course focuses on the local geography, culture, and everyday conversations that are essential for effective communication in Thanesar, Pipli, University Area, and Near Railway Station.

Course Objectives

* Develop confidence in speaking English in everyday situations * Improve pronunciation and fluency in spoken English * Enhance vocabulary related to local geography, culture, and daily life * Learn essential phrases and expressions for effective communication * Improve listening and speaking skills through interactive sessions

Course Structure

The Spoken English 60 Days Course is divided into 60 sessions, each lasting 60 minutes. The course is structured into three phases: ### Phase 1: Foundations of Spoken English (Days 1-20) * **Day 1-5:** Introduction to Spoken English, Basic Phrases, and Greetings + Learn basic phrases and greetings in English + Understand the importance of pronunciation and intonation + Practice basic conversations with peers and instructors * **Day 6-10:** Vocabulary Building and Sentence Structure + Learn vocabulary related to local geography and culture + Understand basic sentence structure and grammar rules + Practice building sentences and short conversations * **Day 11-15:** Listening and Speaking Skills + Improve listening skills through audio and video materials + Practice speaking skills through role-plays and group discussions + Learn essential phrases for everyday conversations * **Day 16-20:** Cultural Immersion and Idioms + Learn idiomatic expressions and phrases related to local culture + Understand cultural nuances and customs in everyday conversations + Practice using idioms and phrases in context

Phase 2: Practical Applications (Days 21-40)

* **Day 21-25:** Everyday Conversations and Interactions + Learn essential phrases for everyday conversations, such as ordering food, asking for directions, and making reservations + Practice using phrases in real-life situations + Improve listening and speaking skills through interactive sessions * **Day 26-30:** Local Geography and Culture + Learn vocabulary related to local landmarks, festivals, and traditions + Understand cultural nuances and customs in everyday conversations + Practice using vocabulary in context * **Day 31-35:** Role-plays and Group Discussions + Practice role-plays and group discussions on various topics + Improve speaking skills through interactive sessions + Learn essential phrases for effective communication * **Day 36-40:** Refining Skills and Confidence Building + Refine speaking skills through feedback and self-assessment + Build confidence in speaking English through interactive sessions + Learn essential phrases for effective communication

Phase 3: Mastery and Fluency (Days 41-60)

* **Day 41-45:** Advanced Vocabulary and Grammar + Learn advanced vocabulary related to local culture and geography + Understand complex grammar rules and sentence structures + Practice using advanced vocabulary and grammar in context * **Day 46-50:** Debates and Presentations + Practice debating and presenting on various topics + Improve speaking skills through interactive sessions + Learn essential phrases for effective communication * **Day 51-55:** Refining Skills and Confidence Building + Refine speaking skills through feedback and self-assessment + Build confidence in speaking English through interactive sessions + Learn essential phrases for effective communication * **Day 56-60:** Final Assessment and Graduation + Participate in a final assessment to evaluate speaking skills + Receive a certificate of completion and graduation + Celebrate achievements and progress

Assessment and Evaluation

* Regular assessments and evaluations to track progress and improvement * Feedback and self-assessment to refine speaking skills * Final assessment to evaluate speaking skills and award a certificate of completion
