Understanding the SSC CGL Examination

Before we dive into the coaching centers, it's essential to have a basic understanding of the SSC CGL examination. The SSC CGL is a three-tier examination that consists of: * Tier 1: Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) on General Awareness, Reasoning, and Quantitative Aptitude * Tier 2: MCQs on Quantitative Aptitude, English Language, and Comprehension * Tier 3: Descriptive Paper on English Language, Essay, and Letter Writing * Tier 4: Computer Proficiency Test (CPT) The examination is conducted for various posts in government departments, including Assistant Audit Officer, Assistant Accounts Officer, and Assistant Section Officer.
